# Privacy policy — Minitel AutoConnect

Minitel AutoConnect helps you renew your connection to the EMSE Wi-Fi portal. The app reads the current Wi-Fi network name to check that you are on the network you selected. iOS requires precise location permission for this Wi-Fi lookup; the app does not read or store your coordinates.

The app stores your portal password in the iPhone Keychain. It stores your username, chosen Wi-Fi name, refresh settings, and last check result locally on your device. When the Wi-Fi name matches, the app sends your portal credentials over HTTPS to `fw-cgcp.emse.fr` to sign in. The app does not send this information to the app developer or to analytics or advertising services.

You can remove locally stored data by deleting the app. Deleting the app may not remove a Keychain item on every iOS installation path; contact the developer if you need deletion instructions for a retained Keychain item. The portal operator controls any information collected by its own login service.
